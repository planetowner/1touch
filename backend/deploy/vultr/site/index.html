Account verification

An email from
1Touch?

Our account emails contain a six-digit verification code for an action you request in 1Touch.

When we send a code

  • Creating an email account
  • Resetting a forgotten password
  • Recovering your username
  • Changing your account email address

Codes expire after 10 minutes. If you didn’t request a code, you can ignore the email or contact us.

Verification emails are sent from

noreply@auth.1touch.football
