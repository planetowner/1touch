1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Account information. Email registration uses an email address, username, nickname, and password. We store a password hash rather than the original password. Community and points. We process profile photographs, posts, comments, live-match chat messages, uploaded images or videos, reports, blocks, favorites, followed teams and players, likes, match-prediction selections, points committed to predictions, results, balances, and points history. Optional connections and notifications. Google Calendar connections use a Google account identifier, encrypted refresh token, app-created calendar identifier, and team subscriptions. Push delivery uses a generated installation identifier, FCM token, platform, locale, notification preferences, and delivery records. In-app notification records include relevant activity and read status.

Support and moderation. If you contact us, we process the contact details and information you include. Reports include the reported content reference, reason, reporter account reference, and moderation outcome. Please avoid sending information that is unnecessary for resolving your request.

Device access. The app requests access to the photo/video library for media selection and permission to display notifications. Google Calendar access is granted through Google authorization and is limited to calendars created by the app. The app does not request access to the device address book or precise GPS location. Device region settings also help choose sign-in suggestions on the device.

Network and technical data. Our API receives IP addresses and request information, such as the requested route, time, and response status. Security rate limiting uses hashed request scopes. This is separate from Google's Mobile Ads SDK, which may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information. We do not claim that non-personalized ads involve no personal information. See the advertising section below.

Sensitive information. Account login information combined with access credentials may be treated as sensitive personal information under California law. We use credentials for authentication and account security. We do not ask for race, religion, health information, or sexual orientation in account fields. Public posts and uploaded media can contain information chosen by their authors; do not publish sensitive information about yourself or others.

Google API data. Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

20. HOSTING AND OPERATIONAL RETENTION

Our application server and account database are hosted using Amazon Web Services Lightsail in Seoul, South Korea. Account verification and recovery emails use Amazon Simple Email Service in the Seoul region. Profile photographs and community media are stored using Cloudflare R2. Cloudflare identifies our R2 bucket location as Asia-Pacific (APAC); this regional designation does not identify a specific storage country. Daily database backup files are subject to a fourteen-day retention schedule. Manual and deployment database backups are subject to a thirty-day retention schedule. Expired backups are removed during scheduled backup processing after a new backup has been successfully created and verified. Information associated with a deleted account may remain in backup copies until they are removed under the applicable retention schedule. Container service logs are rotated according to storage size rather than a fixed number of days. Account deletion and the treatment of published community content are described in the account deletion section of this policy. Contact support@1touch.football for a request concerning your personal information in retained copies.
